I am a software engineer with an interest in philosophy and literature, among other subjects.

I was formerly a Seventh-day Adventist. The essays published on this site---as well as
on other sites like *Spectrum* and *Adventist Today*---were written between
2020 and 2023, as I was still reexamining my faith. I have decided to publish these articles
even though I am no longer interested in writing for an Adventist audience because I invested
a lot of time in writing them. I feel that they mark an important turning point of my life.
